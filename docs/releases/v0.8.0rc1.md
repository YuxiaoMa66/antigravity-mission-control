# v0.8.0rc1: Routing for Gemini 4 Argon and Claude 5.5 (pre-release)

This release candidate updates model routing for the AGY catalog that arrived with AGY 1.4.2. Job handling, approvals and the Skill workflow are unchanged. npm version: `0.8.0-rc.1`.

**What changed in AGY**

- `agy models` on AGY 1.4.2 lists `gemini-4-argon-low`, `-medium` and `-high`, and Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5, each in `-low`, `-medium` and `-high` variants. Claude Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6 are gone from the list.
- On that catalog v0.7.0 could not propose Argon at all: its non-Flash Gemini pattern only matched `gemini-*-pro-high`. Strategy B proposed `claude-opus-5-5-low` for the scout, planner and reviewer, because all three Claude 5.5 variants share one version number and the first one listed won.

**Changes**

- Non-Flash Gemini High (today `gemini-4-argon-high`) takes the place Pro High had in strategies A and B.
- Strategy B planner and reviewer proposals try non-Flash Gemini High first, then Claude Opus, then Claude Sonnet. When both Argon and Opus are available, the host offers a per-role choice between them for the planner and the reviewer; Argon is the default.
- Strategy B scout proposals now follow the implementer order: Gemini Flash High, Medium, Low, then non-Flash Gemini High, then Claude (Sonnet before Opus). A scout answers a small read-only question and its findings pass through the planner and reviewer.
- Claude `-low` and `-medium` variants are left out of automatic proposals, as non-High Gemini variants other than Flash already were. You can still pin one in an approved roster.
- Strategy C is unchanged: every role uses the newest Gemini Flash High.
- `scripts/real_agy_check.py --model` now runs models other than Flash High under strategy B; before, every run used strategy C, which refused them.

**Verified on the real AGY CLI**

- `scripts/real_agy_check.py` against AGY 1.4.2 with `gemini-3.8-flash-high`: two consecutive full runs, 19 of 19 each. A foreground run with `--model gemini-4-argon-high` also passes.
- `agy-mc select` against the live AGY 1.4.2 catalog: strategy B gives `gemini-3.8-flash-high` for the scout and implementer and `gemini-4-argon-high` for the planner and reviewer; with `--avoid-family gemini` the planner, implementer and reviewer get `claude-opus-5-5-high` and the scout gets `claude-sonnet-5-5-high`.

Try it with `npx antigravity-mission-control@next install`, or `python3 -m pip install "git+https://github.com/YuxiaoMa66/antigravity-mission-control.git@v0.8.0rc1"`. Stable remains v0.7.0 and `@latest` does not move. Validation: 118 Python tests, 13 npm tests, and 19 of 19 real-AGY scenarios.
